# Scenes Gathered Zine — genuine compositional revision

Skill: scenes-gathered-zine-v1-3, followed by built-in ImageGen.

## Scene card

- Core: the seated couple at the right, and the petal-covered gown sweeping left.
- Supporting scenery: maritime mural, sofa, column and dark window.
- Invariants: original people/pose/gaze/veil/clothing/train; sofa behind them;
  window on the right; no portrait cutouts or second photograph.
- Dominant gesture: the gown spreads leftward; architecture rises at the right.
- Native colour: warm gray and ivory, wood brown, dark window. No added red.

## Abstraction map

- Retain: only the mural's cloud mass and the room's loose architectural direction.
- Merge: all cloud wisps into one broad, broken gray printed field.
- Omit: 70–80% of cloud microdetail, ceiling decoration, repeated frame lines;
  absolutely no added boats, sea horizon or maritime illustration.
- Transform: the upper mural becomes a spacious ink field, not a photo filter.
- Expose: off-white fibre paper through and around the gray field, not an empty
  decorative border. Two interrupted architecture contours can be a support.

After visual feedback: the first large diagonal paper shape dominated and
pressed the photograph down. Move the factual anchor up 120px; expand the
photograph to about 57–60% of the poster. Lift the environmental boundary much
farther from the couple; make the paper transition thin and interrupted instead
of a white foreground flap. Use only a few airy cloud gestures, with 65–75% quiet
paper within the illustrated area. The matte follows ROOM geometry, not anatomy,
and leaves generous original background around faces and veil.

Keep “In the same light” small and quiet. No large title, extra icons, date or
second image. User constraints override the skill's added high-chroma default:
no new saturated accent. Reference the existing page-two paper for colour/grain.

Image generation creates the illustration and paper seam. Original photographic
RGB is restored across the protected region before delivery; source hash and
every protected output pixel are verified. Local preview only, not OSS.
