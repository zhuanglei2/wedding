# OMM lower-panel generation

Built-in image generation, not CLI/API fallback. The original photograph is never regenerated.

## Initial prompt

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset: ONLY the landscape 3:2 LOWER illustration panel for an OMM photo-and-acrylic diptych. Produce 1536 x 1024 if possible. Do NOT make a complete poster and do NOT include any photograph.

Input 1 is the user's NEW portrait wedding photograph, the sole source of subjects, gestures, environment and palette. Input 2 is a blank-paper base taken from page two of the user's invitation; preserve its cream-white tone and tactile pressed fibrous grain across this entire panel, drawing only a small concentrated artwork around the center. No new parchment, yellowing, background vignette, margins, frame or paper shadows.

Draw a tiny sparse, asymmetrical, poetic matte acrylic observation: the groom stands at LEFT with a narrow near-black tuxedo mass, his hands together in front and one foot gently crossed. The bride stands at RIGHT, slightly angled but looking towards us, holding a very small ivory bouquet with a muted olive stroke. Her ivory gown forms a broad sweeping triangular mass with a long flowing hem. Retain the open SPACE BETWEEN them from the source; do not turn them into a hugging couple. Render small abstract heads with minimal or absent facial marks, never a detailed face, cartoon portrait or realistic miniature.
From the actual painting behind them take ONLY a faint broken horizontal harbor line and two or three wavering mast/hull marks suggesting the ship, behind the central gap; omit the large rectangular picture frame, molding, clouds and realistic room.

The ENTIRE illustrated group, including the ship cue, must fit within roughly 35% of the panel width and 34% of its height (around 12% of the panel area), slightly below the optical center. Large continuous unmarked paper must dominate above, below and on both sides. Do not enlarge the characters to fill the panel.
Use fine slightly unstable charcoal/taupe hand-drawn lines and a FEW flat opaque acrylic masses with irregular dry-brush breaks and grain showing through. No contour tracing, no realistic fabric-fold modeling. Compress the gown into two or three broad creamy ivory paint shapes. Palette maximum FOUR dominant paints from the original: warm charcoal, creamy ivory, muted taupe, muted olive. Paper-white supports them. Gentle vintage artist-book feeling, tactile and restrained rather than decorative or glossy.
No text, no lettering, no dates, no caption, no signatures, no watermark, no ornaments or hearts, no new objects or people, no gradients, no watercolor wash, no shadows, no vector finish.
The actual source photo will be placed UNCHANGED in the upper panel later using deterministic composition, so do not regenerate or photographically reproduce it.
